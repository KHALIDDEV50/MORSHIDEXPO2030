# Destination photo credits

Photos in `public/images/places/` are 1280 px renditions downloaded once (2026-10-12) from Wikimedia Commons.
Only files with a free license were used: CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA.
Each photo is credited in the app: on the place details page and on the place card over the map, linked to the file page.

**Not used:** official tourism galleries (Visit Saudi, the Royal Commission for AlUla). Their media terms do not grant redistribution, and permission would be needed first.

**CC BY-SA files:** adapted versions must stay under the same license. These are resized only, which is not an adaptation of the content.

**No photo yet (labelled placeholders):** Souq Al-Zal, Al-Bujairi Terrace, King Abdullah Park and Al-Soudah Park. No suitable free-licensed photo was found.
